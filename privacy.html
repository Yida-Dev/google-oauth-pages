Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

What this application accesses

Personal Google API Auth accesses Google user data only after the account owner explicitly grants access on Google's consent screen. The data is limited to the owner's own Gmail, Google Analytics, Search Console, Site Verification and Indexing data.

How the data is used

The data is used only for the owner's personal automation: reading verification emails, creating and reading analytics properties, registering and verifying the owner's websites, submitting sitemaps and indexing notifications.

Storage and sharing

The application does not collect Google user data into any database, does not store it beyond what a single automated task needs, and does not share, sell or transfer it to any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings under Security, Third-party apps and services.
